# Bluetooth MIDI in 1.8: the measurement and the plan

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-05. Step 2 of [the Bluetooth research](2026-11-05-bluetooth-research.md)
(the measurement build) is done; the rest is a plan for [decision 0011](../decisions/0011-bluetooth-midi.md).
Nothing here ran on an FM-1, and no MIDI, USB or Bluetooth port was opened. The owner decides the
items at the end before any step after step 1 starts.

Tags as in the research: **[measured]** built or run here; **[verified]** read in the source at
the commit named; **[estimated]** reasoned, with the basis stated.

## The answer in short

- **Bluetooth MIDI both ways costs 39,312 bytes of flash** [measured] (peripheral only 30,704;
  with pairing 45,780), 3,060 bytes of the RAM region, 3,928 of NOINIT and 32 of RAMTEXT. The audio
  interrupt's cost is unchanged to the instruction with the radio off; the radio's own interrupts
  cannot run in fm1-emu.
- **It does not fit.** Today 15,616 bytes are free under the 568,000-byte ceiling. Both ways is
  23,696 over it, 10,132 over the app slot itself. The slot cannot grow: the loader takes a package
  only if its flash image is exactly `[0, 0x93000)`.
- **Decision 0011's first source of room is mostly spent.** The cold files at `-Oz` were worth
  about 6.8 KB in the flash study; 1.5 to 1.7 took most of it, and about 1.3 KB is left.
- **Recommended:** release the ceiling's reserve (13,564), take USB audio to 44.1 kHz only (8,256),
  and the small build wins (about 4 KB). That fits the peripheral with about 8 KB to spare. Both ways
  is 1.2-2.7 KB short of that, which the radio driver at plain `-Os` (3,264, an upstream question)
  or one hot/cold file split would cover. The RAM region needs about 920 bytes, from moving the
  MIDI input ring to NOINIT. The owner decides (the end of this document).
- **felucca-bsp:** PR #21 is open with no review and now conflicts with main in a comment. With it
  merged on today's main, SIDOU passes 23 of 23 USB tests after a one-line change in
  `usb_audio.c`.

## 1. The measurement build

`-DSIDOU_BLE=ON` ([BUILDING.md](../../BUILDING.md), [the design notes](../design-notes.md#bluetooths-measurement-build-sidou_ble)):

- the stack's host side in its own unit (`platform/fm1/ble_stack_unit.c`, fm1-base's `-Os`);
- the radio driver (`ble_hw_wl82.c`, felucca-bsp's `fm1_ble.h`) and SIDOU's glue
  (`platform/fm1/ble_radio.c`) in `main.c`'s unit, which owns Felucca's HAL;
- the glue: the address (random static), MIDI rings both ways, the radio's start, and in the central
  role the scan table and a connection to the first BLE-MIDI device found; BLE MIDI comes in as USB
  packets (`midi.c`'s input ring) and SIDOU's MIDI out goes to the link as well. The radio never
  starts (`ble_radio_wanted` is never set), so the build runs in fm1-emu;
- the stack's state, the rings and the scan table in NOINIT; the DMA block and the PLL table in RAM.

With the option off the default image is byte-identical: `sidou_app.bin` SHA-256 `78a01a45…`, 552,384
bytes, before and after [measured].

- **Against the research's estimate:** peripheral 30.7 KB in the 29-33 KB estimated, both ways 39.3 KB
  below the 41-53 KB. RAM came in lower than the 8-10 KB because the stack's state went to NOINIT.
- **Where both ways' 39,312 bytes are:** the stack's unit about 13.1 KB (link layer, host, ATT,
  BLE-MIDI, scan, the client); the driver, the radio's start-up and calibration and the glue about
  26.2 KB: the captured start-up program `ble_rf_prog` 5,795, `fm1_ble_rf_exec` 4,336, the
  calibration `fm1_rfc_before_187` 2,964, the two interrupts 2,818, the glue's poll 1,570, scanning
  and initiating about 2.4 KB, the AGC table 512.
- **RAMTEXT:** +32, the crystal ramp `fm1_rf_burst_run` (36 bytes, which must run from SRAM), inside the
  16-byte alignment; 6,368 left.
- **RAM region:** 4,204 free with both ways, under emu-gate's `REGION_FREE_MIN` (5,120) by 916. The
  radio's DMA reads `bb` (1,508) and the PLL table (972), so they stay; the driver's other state is
  about 600 bytes. `a_tunes_memory_fits_m3s_budgets` fails on it; FREE_RAM_MIN (9,532 of 8,976) holds.
- **Variants measured on the way:** the stack inside `main.c`'s unit, 598,480 (+6,784: `main.c`'s
  `-inline-threshold=225`); the stack at `-Oz` (fm1-base's `unity_opt = "z"`), 448 less (peripheral)
  and 912 less (both ways); `main.c` at plain `-Os`, 5,424 less with Bluetooth (2,160 of it `main.c`'s
  own, 3,264 the driver's).

### Time [measured, fm1-emu; the radio off]

- emu-gate's boot, `ram_audio`, `sid_budget`, `fx_budget`, `slots_budget` and `psid_budget` on the
  Bluetooth build: **21 of 23 pass**; the two that fail are the flash ceiling and the RAM region,
  above.
- **The audio interrupt is unchanged to the instruction:** one chord 396.64 instructions a sample with
  the tap and 392.69 without, the two-chip and effect benches, the worst `.sid` blocks (55.1 a sample
  on `ghosttrackers.sng`) are the default build's numbers exactly. Nothing of Bluetooth runs in it.
- **The main loop:** 165,572 more instructions in `boot.rs`'s 900 ms run (+0.9 % of the run; about
  0.08 % of the core), from `ble_radio_poll` and the input pump each pass.
- **With the radio on** nothing can be measured here: fm1-emu has no radio. Optimist's emulator
  measured the stack's interrupts at about 0.02 % of the core, below the audio
  ([the research](2026-11-05-bluetooth-research.md#3-cpu-and-timing)).

## 2. Where the room could come from

What both ways needs, beyond today's 15,616 free: 23,696 for the measured build, plus the rest of the
feature (the SETTINGS row and its popup, the DEVICES page, the address in the store, the port in the
routing: about 2.5-4 KB [estimated, from SIDOU's views of the same size]), plus the felucca-bsp move
(+784, measured below): **about 27-28.5 KB**. The peripheral alone: about 17-18 KB.

rest of the feature (about 1.2 KB for the peripheral, 2.5-4 KB both ways).

**Recommendation (the owner decides):** R1 and R2 now, R4, R5 and R7 as engineering, and RAM through
NOINIT (below). That ships the peripheral with room for 1.8's small items. For both ways in 1.8 as
decision 0011 asks, file row 18 and take R6 if felucca-bsp agrees, or do R8's first split
(`sid_character.c`'s cold half); if neither lands in time, the peripheral goes first and connecting
out follows in the next release. Without R2, both ways needs R10.

**RAM:** about 920 bytes for the region's floor. `midi.c`'s input ring `in_ring` (3,088 bytes, read
and written by the CPU only) can move to NOINIT, zeroed in `midi_init`, the same rule as the stack's
state: a placement, not a budget change. The other choice is the owner lowering `REGION_FREE_MIN`.

## 3. The felucca-bsp trial

Re-run in a scratch build on SIDOU `a2a5c767` with felucca-bsp main `41a6e86` plus PR #21 [measured]:

- **PR #21**: open, no review or comment, last updated 2026-10-31. It now conflicts with main in
  `usb.c`'s head comment (#25's `FELUCCA_MSC` paragraph landed beside ours); the hook's code applies
  as it is. Ours to rebase. **#15** open, no reply. **#16**: fixed in `14610bd`, waits on our long
  48 kHz recording on an FM-1 (a device step). **#14** closed.
- **On main since `773b0b9`** (18 commits): in `chip/usb.c` a lone SysEx byte joins the frame
  (`80db9c9`), new hooks `FELUCCA_SYSEX_BYTE`, `FELUCCA_USB_HOST_NAME`, `FELUCCA_MIDI_STAMP`; in the HAL
  SIDOU uses, `fm1_usb.h` casts and two `fm1_input.h` changes (the battery in the key scan,
  `fm1_led_dim_ns`); `chip/ota.c` gains M-UPGRADE 3.11's handshake. Nothing touches `fm1_sys.h`, the
  radio files or the licences. **PR #20** (SysEx frames queued, open) would remove `sx_ready` and
  `sx_frame_len`, which SIDOU's `midi.c` reads (row 16).
- **`chip/usb.c` in place of 1.6.1's:** image 552,560 (+176), RAMTEXT unchanged, the loader unchanged.
  `FELUCCA_UAC_AS_FIRST` must be set to 1 (felucca-bsp's default became 0). **23 of 23** USB and
  MIDI-transport tests pass after two changes:
  - `usb_audio.c` hands the recording 24-bit words (`b->frames[i] = half[i]`, the `>> 8` dropped):
    since `6a1fd30` `uac_tap` takes I2S words, and SIDOU's 16-bit samples recorded 48 dB low (the one
    failure, `a_host_records_the_dacs_output_over_usb_audio_at_44_1_and_48_khz`). The test's bit-exact
    expectation stays: SIDOU's DAC words are 16-bit samples times 256, so the new rounding gives the
    same values. 16 bytes less.
  - emu-gate's USB helper read the manufacturer string from usb.c's `STR1`, which is gone (the
    descriptor is built on request): it must read the descriptor the device sends instead.
- **felucca-bsp's HAL in place of 1.6.1's:** one compile error (`fm1_cpu1_halt` is now
  `fm1_cpu1_stop`, `fm1_cpu1_stop.h`); image 553,168 (+784 with `usb.c`), NOINIT 400 (+320,
  `fm1_crash_ctx`), RAMTEXT unchanged. The loader builds with the HAL too, so it would change
  (8,216 to 8,424 bytes) and lose its pinned Felucca 1.6.1 SHA-256: the loader keeps 1.6.1's HAL, or
  moves with a new pin. The emulator tests were not run on this build.
- Upstream rows 15 to 17 record the porting note, PR #20's question and two warnings.

## 4. The emulator and the radio

- **fm1-emu models no radio** [verified, `fm1-soc` at `95343aa`]: the baseband's window reads 0 and
  takes writes. Started at boot there, SIDOU's radio start-up runs to its time-outs without a hang or
  a fault and SIDOU plays, but the start holds the main loop past 900 ms (`boot.rs`'s held key misses
  its window) [measured].
- **What fm1-emu can prove:** the sizes and RAM placement; the audio and main-loop budgets with the
  stack linked; the boot with the radio off and on; the start-up's main-loop hold and its
  interrupts-off time, as a budget; and the glue end to end without the air: a test writes MIDI
  packets into `ble_radio_in` and reads `ble_radio_out` by symbol, so a note from "Bluetooth" sounds,
  a dropped link releases held notes, and SIDOU's MIDI out reaches the link's ring.
- **What it cannot:** advertising, connecting, the link's timing, scanning, the central's GATT client.
- **Optimist's fork (w0ts/fm1-emulator, GPL-3.0)** models the engine with virtual centrals and
  peripherals; SIDOU 1.6.0 did not boot there (its key matrix, the research). Two ways forward: make
  SIDOU boot in the fork (a test-only harness, nothing linked into SIDOU), or ask fm1-emu (MIT) for an
  engine model, which needs Optimist's agreement to port GPL code or a reimplementation from the fact
  sheet. The first is in SIDOU's hands.
- **Host tests:** the stack's own (fm1-base `tests/bt`, 33 binaries with a fake engine) as they are;
  SIDOU's glue in `sidou-tests`.

### The owner's device test

Each item waits for the owner's yes for that step; nothing is flashed or connected until then.

1. **The boot with felucca-bsp's raised rails** (step 1b): power on ten times, a tune, a save.
2. **BLUETOOTH ON:** the radio starts within a few seconds of the setting, the sound keeps playing,
   no reboot; OFF stops it, a power cycle keeps the setting.
3. **SIDOU seen from a Mac:** Audio MIDI Setup, Window, Show MIDI Studio, the Bluetooth button: the
   Bluetooth Configuration window lists `SIDOU XXXX`; Connect. Notes from GarageBand or Logic play
   SIDOU; SIDOU's keys and knobs arrive in the DAW; SIDOU follows the DAW's clock and start / stop.
   Disconnect while a note is held: no stuck note.
4. **SIDOU connecting out to the microKEY Air:** on DEVICES, scan, pick the microKEY Air, connect;
   its keys play SIDOU. Write down whether it asks for pairing (then step 10's pairing comes first)
   and whether a computer can connect to SIDOU while it is linked (one link at a time: it cannot).
5. **An hour of two SIDs** with Bluetooth MIDI in from the Mac: no clicks, no reboot.
6. **A save during a link** (the store's erase holds up to 35 ms): the link survives or comes back.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Flash:** release the 13,564 bytes above the ceiling to Bluetooth (R1)? Decision 0011's point 2
   keeps them untouched, so this revisits it. The felucca-bsp move needs 784 of the slot; nothing
   else claims it.
2. **USB audio at 44.1 kHz only** (R2, 8,256 bytes)? Without it, both ways needs a product cut.
3. **Both ways in 1.8, or the peripheral first** with connecting out in the next release, if R6 or
   R8 do not come in time? Decision 0011 says both ways; the measurement says it is 1.2-2.7 KB short
   with R1, R2 and the small wins.
4. **RAM:** `midi.c`'s input ring to NOINIT, or a lower `REGION_FREE_MIN`?
5. **Upstream:** rebase PR #21 now; file rows 15 to 18 (and 13) in the next batch after review.
6. **The loader** after the move: keep Felucca 1.6.1's HAL for it (its SHA-256 stays), or move it
   with felucca-bsp and pin its new hash?
7. **The emulator for the air:** make SIDOU boot in Optimist's fork (step 7), or ask fm1-emu first?
8. **The long 48 kHz recording** felucca-bsp#16 waits on: run it on the device (needs the owner's
   yes), or answer that SIDOU moves to 44.1 kHz only if R2 is taken.
9. **The site's wasm core** is 164,811 bytes on v1.7.0, over `web/core.test.mjs`'s 160 KiB budget,
   so the merge check's web stage fails on 1.7.0 as it is (not from this branch): raise the budget
   or win the bytes back?
