**Goal:** [LAB's design](2026-10-25-lab-design.md), exactly: a view where one LIVE sound is shaped
on five pages (OSC, ENVELOPE, FILTER, MOD, FX), each with its shape drawn from the live values
above four cards for K1-K4, and kept with one press of SAVE into 16 USER sound slots; A/B, undo
and two variation rolls to experiment with; PRESETS on LIVE stepping on into USER 1-16; the unsaved
edit back at power-on. On LIVE, ENV, LFO and FX open LAB at their page. Ergonomics first: what is
turned is lit, what it does is drawn, and every LAB gesture is on the screen's hint line.

**Out of this plan** (the design's "Later" and its second step): the song bridge (a sound to and
from a GoatTracker instrument), hold-to-peek, macros, a FAV/RECENT browser, A-B morph, program
changes to USER sounds.

**Architecture:** LAB is one new view (`app/lab_view.c`) on the views' stack over LIVE, with a page
byte and its own input; it turns parameters `live` already has (plus three new ones: PWM depth,
PWM rate, fine tune), so the audio path and RAMTEXT do not change. A sound is a packed 45-byte
image of the voice and the three effect slots (`app/lab_sound.c`); `live` keeps the current
sound's base (as loaded or kept) in RAM so a USER sound is a preset like the built-ins. The 16
USER sounds are one store chunk with its own format version, and the unsaved edit is one more
small record written by the settings' 5 s debounce (`app/user_sound.c`, modelled on
`app/user_profile.c`). The drawings are pixel overlays on the grid's cells (as LIVE's scopes) under
text built in the song views' shared canvas (as CHIP), so they cost no RAM of their own. Undo is a
ring of (what, old value) entries in which a roll is one step; A/B swaps the live sound with one
45-byte copy.

**Tech:** C11, no allocation, no libm; host tests in Rust through `sidou-sys` (bindgen); fm1-emu
for the device tests (`emu-gate`), the ergonomics harness (`ergo`), the browser demo.

## The design, settled for the code

What the design leaves to the plan, decided here within its decisions. Items marked
**(asks)** are put to the user at Task 1's stop with the proposal shown; the tasks are written

### The sound

A sound is what the design's "What a sound holds" lists, as one packed image (`lab_sound`,
45 bytes, little-endian, `app/lab_sound.h`):

| Offset | Bytes | Field |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | control: waveform, ring and sync bits of `$d404` (never the gate) |
| 1, 2 | 1 + 1 | attack/decay, sustain/release |
| 3 | 2 | pulse width, 0..4095 |
| 5 | 2 | cutoff, 0..2047 |
| 7, 8, 9 | 1 each | resonance, filter voices (`$d417` bits 0-2), filter mode (`$d418` bits 4-6) |
| 10 | 1 | mono sync (1: SYNC LEAD's one-voice sync) |
| 11 | 2 | the sync ratio's base, in 1/256 |
| 13 | 1 | the sync ratio's offset in semitones (signed) |
| 14 | 2 | PWM depth, pulse-width steps |
| 16 | 2 | PWM rate, LFO phase step a tick |
| 18 | 1 | fine tune, cents, -50..+50 (signed) |
| 19..25 | 7 | bend range, velocity, mod and pressure targets, vibrato rate, depth, delay |
| 26..29 | 4 | ARP on, rate, order, span |
| 30 | 15 | the three slots' algorithm, Amount, A, B, Mix |

Not in it, as the design says: the chip's profile and character, the model, the envelope mode
(instrument-wide), the octave, the volume.

- **The base:** `live` holds the current sound's base, the sound as loaded or kept, and its name
  (`live.base`, `live.base_name`). `load_preset` fills it from the factory preset (the slots as
  they are: a built-in has none of its own), a USER sound from its slot, DICE from its roll; a keep
  sets it to what was kept. `cur(l)`'s reads become reads of the base, so a USER sound is a preset
  like the others. The edited mark `*` is the live sound differing from the base. **(asks)** The
  slots count for `*` on LIVE too (today they do not), since a keep saves them.
- **Loading** a USER sound sets the slots; loading a built-in leaves them as they are (today's
  rule). **(asks)**
- **A USER sound's edits** when PRESETS steps away: one shared kept edit (a packed sound and its
  slot, 46 bytes, on top of the table's total) for the USER sound last left; the 16 slots do not
  each keep one (16 x 46 = 736 bytes). **(asks)**

### The pages

| Page | K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 | ALGORITHM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OSC | `PULSE_WIDTH` | `PWM_DEPTH` (new) | `PWM_RATE` (new) | `TUNE` (new) | TRI, SAW, PUL, NOI, TRI+SAW, TRI+PUL, SAW+PUL, T+S+P, TRI RING, SAW SYNC, PUL SYNC (`WAVEFORM`, `RING`, `SYNC`) |
| ENVELOPE | `ATTACK` | `DECAY` | `SUSTAIN` | `RELEASE` | `ENVELOPE_MODE` (INSTANT, AUTHENTIC; marked ALL SOUNDS) |
| FILTER | `CUTOFF` (K1's walk, as LIVE) | `RESONANCE` | `FILTER_VOICES` (0..7, drawn as V1 V2 V3) | nothing until Task 18 | LP, BP, HP, NOTCH (`FILTER_MODE` 1, 2, 4, 5) |
| MOD | `VIBRATO_RATE` | `VIBRATO_DEPTH` | `VIBRATO_DELAY` | `VELOCITY_TARGET` until Task 18 | ARP OFF, UP, DOWN, UP-DOWN, PLAYED (`live_arp_set`) |
| FX | the selected slot's Amount | A | B | Mix | the slot's algorithm, OFF included |

The OSC list is the design's "the waveform, the combined ones, then +RING, +SYNC" as one flat list
(ring with the triangle, sync with saw and pulse, as DICE pairs them). On a mono-sync sound without
the pulse bit OSC's K1 reads RATIO and turns the sync ratio's semitones (LIVE's K3 on SYNC LEAD),
since a pulse width does nothing there. **(asks)**

The cards' detents: as LIVE where LIVE has the control (K1's cutoff walk, 0..15 a step a detent,
slots by `LIVE_SLOT_DETENT`), PWM depth 32 steps a detent (0..2048), PWM rate 1 (0..255 in
0.05 Hz: PWM BASS's 328 is 10), tune 1 cent, vibrato 1 (0..127).

### Controls in LAB (the design's table, with the gaps closed)

- **EDIT's tap** is A/B: EDIT's release with nothing else pressed or turned while it was down
  (note keys do not spoil it, as for SAVE). A knob turned while A shows makes A the edit: B's
  difference from it becomes one undo step, so nothing is lost. **(asks)** On an unedited sound the
  tap toasts `NOTHING TO COMPARE`.
- **EDIT + K** in LAB turns the card as without EDIT (no hidden combinations: the audit's rule) and
  spoils the tap. **EDIT + PRESETS** reverts to the base, as on LIVE, one undo step.
- **ARP alone** in LAB switches the chord arpeggio at its release, as on LIVE. **(asks)**
- **SCL** on any page opens FX on the next slot (on FX: the next slot).
- **ENV, LFO, FX** open their page; on their own page they do nothing.
- **HOME** goes back to LIVE: the menu's LAB item replaces the menu (pops it, pushes LAB), so HOME
  from LAB is always LIVE, and the stack never holds MENU and LAB.
- **Undo steps:** one detent or a run of detents on one card (until another control is used) is
  one step; an ALGORITHM run is one step; a roll, a revert, an A/B edit (above) and a PRESETS load
  inside LAB are one step each. A load resets the history (undoing past a load would mix sounds):
  the history and A/B live until another sound loads, across HOME and back.
- **The name page** (EDIT + SAVE) is a mode of LAB, not a pushed view (the stack's depth): K1 the
  slot (`U01`..`U16`, a used slot showing its name), the note keys type A..Z and the 27th key a
  space at the cursor and step it on, SELECT moves the cursor, ALGORITHM changes the character
  under it (SAVE AS's alphabet), SAVE writes, a used slot asks once more (`OVERWRITE U03? SAVE`),
  HOME leaves without writing. **(asks)** (the design's "the keys the name").
- **A full set** of 16: SAVE on a sound that is not a USER sound toasts `USER FULL: EDIT+SAVE`
  and writes nothing. **(asks)**
- **The automatic name** is `LAB nn` with nn the slot (`KEPT AS U03 LAB 03`).

### Outside LAB after the LIVE change

`panel_buttons` loses FX and ENV: on LIVE they open LAB (as LFO), in LAB they are pages, and in
the other views they do nothing (they were the chip toggle and next algorithm everywhere).
**(asks)** SCL stays "next slot" everywhere. FX's LED keeps showing the 8580.

### The store

- **The 16 sounds** as one chunk with its own format version: **(asks, the RAM question)** either
  a **file** (type `STORE_FILE_SOUNDS` 4, private like `STORE_FILE_GTI`, a reserved name
  `USER SOUNDS` that SAVE AS and SysEx refuse; written by streaming through the store's 256-byte
  staging buffer from the old file and the new slot, **0 bytes of RAM**; read a slot at a time with
  `store_file_read`; a keep claims a sector, 3,744 bytes, as a song save does) **(proposed)**, or a
  **small value** (key `0x10A`, 931 bytes, its write buffer 931 bytes of RAM; read with a new
  `store_get_at(key, offset, ...)` so no 1,248-byte stack copy is needed; a keep appends 951
  bytes to the head sector).
- **The chunk's layout (either form):** `[version 1][sound bytes 45][slots 16]` then per slot
  `[used][name 12][sound]`; a later version appends to each sound (the stride is
  `1 + 12 + sound bytes`), so this firmware reads a later chunk's first 45 bytes of each.
- **The unsaved edit:** key `0x109` (type 4), `[version][preset: 0..4 a built-in, 5 + n USER n][edited][name 12][sound bytes][sound]`,
  61 bytes, written by the settings' debounce after the settings record (the same wake-up, the same
  5 s) and applied at boot after it. The settings record's preset byte holds `5 + n` for a USER
  sound (an older firmware clamps it to DICE).
- **An older firmware ignores both:** an unknown key is never read, an unknown file type is never
  listed (SONG lists songs, SIDS tunes); a newer chunk version's appended bytes are skipped.

## Risks

- **Flash** is the tightest budget: 7,360 bytes under M3's ceiling against LAB's 11,000-14,000.
  Without Task 1b's stage or a win-back, Task 6 or 7 will not fit. Every task prints the image.
- **The input gates:** `knob_page` and `shown()` are inlined into the encoder's input for LIVE's
  and CHIP's bounds (`POPUP_INPUT` 250, `CHIP_INPUT` 215, the M3 plan's 201 of 215); LAB's branch
  must not cost them their margin (Task 5 measures; a LAB test is a byte compare on the stack's
  top view, after LIVE's).
- **The drawings' cost:** a curve recomputed every frame while the envelope moves; the kit
  updates only the cells that change and holds 200 cells a frame (Task 6's budget test is the
  gate); overlays are shared with LIVE's scopes (255 slots, LIVE frees them when covered).
- **The store:** a file form claims a sector per keep and is one writer at a time (a SysEx
  upload holds a keep back: it retries); its reserved name must be refused by SAVE AS and SysEx;
  the small-value form costs 931 bytes of RAM. The 48 keys: the record takes one, the chunk one.
- **Settings schema 8** (Task 3) and the record's boot order (Task 4): the old-record fixtures must
  keep loading, and a USER sound's preset byte must load an empty slot safely.
- **The LIVE change** touches tests across both crates and the ergo tables; it lands as one commit
  (Task 16) so `main` never has half of it.
- **`LIVE_PARAM_COUNT` and `midi_out`'s 64-bit mask:** 59 after Task 3, 61 with Task 18's
  proposals; 64 is the ceiling (`PRESET_LOADS`).
- **A/B and EDIT:** EDIT is the slot layer everywhere; LAB's tap must not change EDIT's meaning
  elsewhere (Task 11 checks LIVE's EDIT + K and CHIP's EDIT + ALGORITHM unchanged).
- **The tap in LAB** (the envelope's dot): the tap's cost to the interrupt is measured
  (`ram_audio.rs`'s `the_live_view_tap_costs_the_audio_interrupt_little`) and is already paid on
  LIVE; LAB only turns it on.
