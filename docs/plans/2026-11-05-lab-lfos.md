# Two free LFOs in LAB: design

**Status:** design for 1.7, 2026-11-05.

## Why

Players ask for free LFOs more than for a modulation matrix (MIDIbox SID, TherapSid, SidStation
all have three to six): an LFO on the pulse width, the pitch or the cutoff, with a sample-and-hold
shape. LAB has the vibrato, the PWM and the filter LFO, each fixed to one target and one shape.

## What the player does

MOD's four cards are taken (the vibrato and the arpeggio's rate), so the LFOs are MOD's second
page, **LFO**, right after MOD. **LFO** pressed in LAB goes to MOD, and pressed again on MOD to
LFO (and back): one button reaches both. SELECT steps through it as through any page.

| Control | Does |
|---|---|
| ALGORITHM | the edited LFO's shape: `LFO OFF`, `TRIANGLE`, `SAW UP`, `SAW DOWN`, `SQUARE`, `S&H` (a new random level each cycle) |
| K1 EDIT | which LFO the page edits: `1` or `2` (the header says `LFO 2`) |
| K2 DEST | where it goes: `PCH` (the pitch of every layer), `PW` (every layer's pulse width), `CUT` (the cutoff), `VOL` (the chip's volume: a tremolo) |
| K3 RATE | 32 detents from 0.05 to 12.75 Hz, even in pitch, as OSC's PWM HZ (`1.3`) |
| K4 DEPTH | `-63` .. `+63`: how far, the sign inverting it; at 63 the pitch swings an octave each way, the width and the cutoff half their range, the volume all of it |

The page draws both LFOs side by side, one cycle each: the edited one bright, the other dim,
an off one flat and dark, with its destination under it and a dot on where it is now. Each card
value is at most three characters (2x or 3x): LAB's cards light instead of the popup.

The LFOs run free (no restart at a note-on), at 100 Hz in `live_tick`, from flash.

## A layer's level: not possible without the audio path

The SID has no per-voice volume. A layer's level would need a gain per voice in the audio
interrupt (`sid.c`, RAMTEXT, 16 bytes free): that is the budget the owner keeps. The sustain level
cannot stand in: the SID's envelope only falls to a lower sustain, it never rises. So VOL is the
chip's master volume (`$d418`'s low nibble), every layer at once; per-layer level is left for the
owner (it needs RAMTEXT).

## Sound format

Two LFOs of 3 bytes after the table: the sound grows from 103 to 109 bytes (still one version
step, 1.6's 68 to 1.7's 109).

| Offset | Bytes | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 103 + 3n | 1 | LFO n+1: bits 0-2 the shape (`0` off, `1` triangle, `2` saw up, `3` saw down, `4` square, `5` S&H), bits 4-5 the destination (`0` pitch, `1` pulse width, `2` cutoff, `3` volume) |
| 104 + 3n | 1 | Rate: the PWM HZ walk's detent, `0..31` |
| 105 + 3n | 1 | Depth, signed, `-63..63` |

## Import

SidStation: the first layer's vibrato LFO (pitch), PWM LFO (pulse) and the filter's LFO (cutoff),
the first two that are set, become LFO 1 and 2, their shape (0..4 read as triangle, saw up, saw
down, square, random: best known, the format is not documented; 5 Flat is skipped), speed
(0..127 onto the 32 detents, best known) and depth (halved to 0..63). Any further LFO stays in the
report. MIDIbox SID V2: its LFOs are modulation paths, which stay reported.

## Budgets (estimate)

- Flash: about 2 KB (engine 0.4, page 1.0, drawing 0.5, import none: host).
- RAM: 6 bytes in each of nine sound copies plus 7 bytes of LFO state: about 65 bytes; the undo
  ring grows by 6 entries a side (110).
- RAMTEXT and the audio path: nothing.
