# Punch a playing song

PUNCH is six effects you play on a song while it plays, the way a DJ throws an echo or drops the
filter: hold a key, hear the effect, let go and the song is back as it was. Nothing you do on
PUNCH is written into the song.

## 1. Open it

- Play a song (**PLAY**) and open any song view: **SEQ** gives STEP, then TRACKER, ORDER, INSTR,
  TABLE.
- Press **FX**. PUNCH opens over the song view and the song plays on.
- **HOME** goes back to the song view; **SEQ** goes on to the next one.

## 2. Hold the pads

The six leftmost note keys are the pads. Hold one for as long as you want the effect, hold
several at once, and let go to undo it. The page shows a held pad reversed and its key lights.

| Key | Pad | What you hear |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MUTE** | the song gone; it runs on silently, so it comes back in time |
| 2 | **FILTER SLAM** | the song's filter shut, resonance up: a dull thud of the low end |
| 3 | **PITCH DIVE** | a tape stop: every note slows to an octave down over half a second |
| 4 | **STUTTER** | the beat from the moment you press repeats; let go and the song is exactly where it would have been, as if it had played on |
| 5 | **ECHO THROW** | the song thrown into a delay; let go and the echoes ring on for a few seconds before your own FX slot comes back |
| 6 | **CHIP SWAP** | the song on the other SID chip: a 6581 song on the 8580, an 8580 song on the 6581 |

Try STUTTER and PITCH DIVE together for a slowing loop, or ECHO THROW and MUTE to leave only the
echoes.

## 3. What else

- The other keys do nothing on PUNCH (the page says **KEYS ARE PADS**): no note sounds and nothing
  is recorded.
- With the song stopped the pads wait (**PLAY TO PUNCH**); **PLAY** starts it from PUNCH, and a pad
  you hold then works at once.
- Hold **HOME** for the help card.

Every detail is in [controls.md](../reference/controls.md#punch).
