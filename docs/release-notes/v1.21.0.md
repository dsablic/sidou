# SIDOU 1.21.0: STEP's GROOVE page, and a .sid tune into a song

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html), with a new *What's new in 1.21*.

## On the FM-1

- **STEP's GROOVE page.** ALGORITHM past the pattern's last page (the header's `PAGE 1 2 3 4 G`)
  opens it, and while a song plays one turn clockwise does. Each knob has one meaning, no keys
  held, every turn in the big popup:
  - **K1 SWING**, the song's: STRAIGHT, LIGHT, SHUFFLE or HARD. A song that plays eighths (SIDOU
    LOOP) swings its eighths, one that plays sixteenths its sixteenths. It is heard within about
    two beats while the song plays, the BPM stays, and it is GoatTracker's funktempo, so the song
    swings the same in GoatTracker.
  - **K2 HITS** and **K3 ROTATE**: that many hits spread evenly over the selected lane
    (Bjorklund's rhythms, as `x..x..x.`), turned a step at a time. They are written as the lane's
    own steps, so its note, locks and slides apply.
  - **K4 RATCHET**: the step picked with a step key plays twice, three or four times in its row,
    with ticks under it on the pattern's pages. x4 needs rows of 8 frames (speed 8 or slower).
    GoatTracker plays it too: the step uses a shared copy of its instrument.
- **The song views' header** says the swing under the BPM.

## On the site

- **A `.sid` tune into a song.** Drop a PSID tune on the
  [Library page](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html), pick the sub-tune and a length,
  and SIDOU's own 6502 runs it in your browser and writes it out as an editable GoatTracker song:
  its tempo and swing, notes, a handful of instruments a voice with their wave, pulse and filter
  tables, and patterns that repeat where the tune does; a two-SID tune becomes a six-voice song.
  The original and the conversion play side by side, the page says how many notes the FM-1 plays
  on time and what it simplified, and the song downloads, opens in the editor or goes to the
  FM-1. Nothing is uploaded, and a conversion keeps the original's copyright.

## Measured

The image is 554,916 bytes, 24,600 under its ceiling of 579,516. RAMTEXT holds 44,880 bytes of
its 48 KB. The site's tune module is 78,619 bytes of its 96 KiB, the core 166,355 of its 176 KiB.

**Tested on a real FM-1** (2026-11-17, hardware-test.md section 41), with this package: an install over the test build with the songs, the keyboard's bond and the art pack kept; 1.21.0 on ABOUT; SWING heard on SIDOU LOOP while it plays and back to STRAIGHT; HITS and ROTATE on the bass lane; a x3 snare ratchet; `neon-highway.sid` converted on the Library page, sent and played; the microKEY Air reconnecting after a power cycle. Swing's first build was found inaudible on SIDOU LOOP on the device and fixed before this package.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.21.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Your songs and settings are kept. Coming from
before 1.20, send RED MOON RITE's art from the
[Library page](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's
`FM-1.fwsc` the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.
