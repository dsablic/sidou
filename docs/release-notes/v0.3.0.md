# SIDOU 0.3.0: C64 music on the FM-1

**Write C64 songs on the M-VAVE FM-1, in GoatTracker 2's own format.** SIDOU's tracker and
step sequencer save GoatTracker `.sng` files, and its player plays them as GoatTracker's own does,
register for register: start a tune on the FM-1, finish it in GoatTracker on your computer, play it
back on the FM-1. Underneath are two SID chips, a 6581 and an 8580, whose character you dial in
like an amp, and a live instrument you can play over a song the moment it boots.

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>. The emulated FM-1 keeps
its flash in your browser, so what you save stays.

## What it does

- **The chip's character, dialled in like an amp.** A 6581 and an 8580, five chip profiles (BRIGHT, DARK, CLASSIC,
  CLEAN, GRITTY) plus eight of your own, and the character of a real chip: age, worn DACs, the
  6581's thump on every note, and filter bleed.
- **GoatTracker 2, both ways.** SIDOU's songs are GoatTracker `.sng` files; a C player on the
  device matches GoatTracker's own 6502 player register for register.
- **STEP:** the 16 keys as a drum machine's steps, numbered by beat, with parameter locks on held
  steps. **TRACKER, ORDER, INSTR and TABLE:** the whole song, edited from the panel.
- **REC from the keys:** play over a looping song and the notes land in the pattern, quantised to
  the row (or with a count-in); K1 and K2 record filter locks. TRACKER follows the playhead while
  you record.
- **LIVE:** four presets that remember your edits, DICE for a new sound at every turn, three
  effect slots (distortion, modulation, FX), and a SCENE of generative visuals that move to the
  music.
- **MIDI:** follows and sends clock, start, stop and song position; live, multitimbral,
  whole-chip and omni modes; program change with banks; every parameter as a CC or NRPN; MIDI
  learn on any knob.
- **SysEx over USB:** songs, instruments and files to and from the FM-1's store, at about
  12 KB/s up and 27 KB/s down.
- **CHIP SPREAD:** SETTINGS → SPREAD separates the two chips in stereo while both sound: a song
  on one chip and your playing on the other sit side by side; a chip playing alone stays centred.
- **Chord arpeggios:** tap ARP on LIVE and a held chord plays as one voice stepping through its
  notes at the frame rate, the C64's classic sound; hold ARP and turn K1 to K3 for rate, order and
  span (or sync it to MIDI clock).
- **Help on the device:** hold HOME for a card that says what every control does in the view
  you're in.

## New since 0.2.0

- **Readable at arm's length:** STEP, ORDER, INSTR, TABLE, TRACKER, CHIP and SAVE AS redrawn in
  larger, higher-contrast text; the small text at the bottom of the screens is gone.
- **A simpler menu:** SAVE opens the highlighted item on every list, the settings live under
  SETTINGS, and SEQ goes back to the song view you used last.
- **SysEx 13 to 22 times faster:** a 64 KB file now takes 5.5 s up and 2.4 s down (was 73 s and
  53 s), and an upload works while you play.
- **Steadier MIDI clock:** a song stays within a millisecond of an external clock over ten
  minutes, also across flash writes, which used to knock it out of step.
- **Fixed:**
  - PWM BASS never goes silent at narrow pulse widths.
  - Parameter locks are never lost at a pattern boundary or to a pending edit.
  - REC lands notes in the right pass of a repeated pattern, and records the pitch you heard on
    a transposed orderlist entry.
  - The song views no longer edit ABOUT's tune.
  - A fast turn on STORE can no longer format a store: SAVE confirms it.
  - No MIDI input is lost when the device is busy.
- **Nothing clips:** a soft limiter after the effects keeps every setting off the rails.
- **More room:** about three times the free RAM for what comes next (`.sid` playback in M3).

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Songs to start with

HIGHWAY, PAPER LANTERNS) are a click away: open <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/songs.html> in

## Install

SIDOU installs like the other FM-1 firmwares, over USB, and it can always go back.

- **From the stock firmware or a Felucca-family firmware:** the Firmware Hub, or Felucca's
  installer: `fm1_install.py sidou-0.3.0.fwsc`.
- **From an earlier SIDOU:** the same installer; it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1". On a Mac,
  if it doesn't, delete the old device in Audio MIDI Setup and replug.
- **Back to the stock firmware:** install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc` the same way. Save the songs you
  want first (SysEx GET): the stock firmware uses the same flash.
- **If a unit won't boot:** switch off, hold OCT- and switch on: the chip's ROM boot mode, from
  which the unbricker restores any package
  ([flash-day.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/flash-day.md)). This is tested
  on a real FM-1.

SIDOU answers the installers as **`FM-1_964`**.

## Known limits

- `.sid` file playback is the next milestone (M3); RSID tunes and PlaySID digis come later.
- The store holds 48 entries: songs, settings and profiles together.
- Uploads while sound plays are paced with short dips in the audio, about 28 s for 64 KB.
