# SIDOU 0.4.0: drums, chance and USB audio

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html).

## New

- **A SID drum kit.** A NEW song comes with eight drums made on the chip's own voices: KICK,
  SNARE, CL HAT, OP HAT, CLAP, TOM LO, TOM HI and RIM, picked by name on a STEP lane. They are
  plain GoatTracker 2 instruments, so the song plays the same in GoatTracker.
- **Digi drums.** Four short sampled drums (DG KICK, DG SNARE, DG CLAP, DG HAT), gritty as a
  C64's, played the way C64 tunes play samples. GoatTracker plays the matching chip drum in their
  place, so the song still opens there.
- **Generative STEP.** Hold a step and turn ALGORITHM to give it a chance to play (100 % down to
  0). EDIT + ARP is MUTATE, a small change to the lane's pattern; EDIT + SCL is DICE, a new
  pattern in a minor pentatonic (or a groove on a drum lane). All of it stays GoatTracker data.
- **Record over the USB cable.** The FM-1 is also a USB audio input, no driver needed, on a Mac,
  Windows, Linux or an iPad: a DAW records what the line out plays, at 44.1 or 48 kHz.
- **The button LEDs show more.** A dim glow means set or available, full light means active now:
  ARP flashes with the arpeggio, PLAY on the beat, REC through the count-in, SAVE while a
  question waits for it; with GLO the keys show which voices sound, and in STEP the keys show the
  page and the playhead.
- **An ARP helper in TABLE.** Press ARP on a wavetable row and type an arpeggio as semitone steps,
  a speed and a loop point; SAVE writes plain GoatTracker wavetable rows.
- **SETTINGS → RESET.** Sounds and settings back to factory, confirmed with SAVE; songs, USER
  chip profiles and MIDI learn mappings stay.
- **Songs to MIDI** on the site: <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/midi.html> turns a `.sng` into a
  `.mid`, a track per voice, timed as GoatTracker plays it.
- **Profile words everywhere:** STATUS says `6581 CLASSIC`, not `6581 R4AR`.
- **About 25 KB of flash won back,** with nothing heard differently, so the next release
  (`.sid` playback) has room.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-0.4.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.

**sidou-0.4.0.fwsc**, identity FM-1_964, SHA-256 `SHA256_HERE`.
