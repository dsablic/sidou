# SIDOU 1.8.0: a sound from a prompt, live .sid tweaks, ACID

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html), with a new *What's new in 1.8*.

## On the FM-1

- **Live tweaks on a playing `.sid`.** In NOW PLAYING, K1 bends the tune's filter cutoff
  (-1024..+1024, 32 a detent), K2 its resonance (-15..+15), K3 picks a voice and K4 gives it
  another waveform (TRI, SAW, PUL or NOI; TUNE'S is its own). Each turn shows the TWEAK popup; a
  TWEAKED line says what is bent. Back at 0 each gives the tune its own value at once, and leaving
  NOW PLAYING, another song or a stop gives everything back. EDIT + a knob is still the effect
  slot's.
- **An ACID lane for DICE.** A lane's role can be ACID (after BASS+DRUMS): DICE writes a bassline
  as on a melodic lane and rolls 1.7's SLIDE and ACCENT locks with it, a slide on about three in
  ten notes followed by a note, an accent on about a third; a new roll clears them where a note
  went. SCENE hears the lane as the bass; an older SIDOU rolls it as a melodic lane.
- **felucca-bsp.** SIDOU builds on felucca-bsp's chip and board code (MPL-2.0) byte for byte, in
  place of the copies of Felucca 1.6.1 it carried; the update loader stays Felucca 1.6.1's, by its
  hash. A firmware update's first request is answered even when it comes right behind one of
  SIDOU's own SysEx frames (it was dropped before).
- **USB audio records at 44.1 kHz only.** The 48 kHz choice and its resampler are gone (about 8 KB
  of flash, room for Bluetooth MIDI): a DAW records at 44.1 kHz, the rate SIDOU plays at.
- A step that slides and is accented keeps the accent's sustain. In 1.7 the slide's held SR
  came after the accent, so such a step played at the song's sustain; the accent's cutoff and
  resonance were right.
- STEP's held step shows its chance and nothing after it. With a chance lock on a held step,
  the `95%` beside HELD STEP could be followed by stray characters; the text now ends at the
  percent sign.
- Sounds kept in 1.7 load unchanged; a 1.7 SIDOU plays a 1.8 sound and song unchanged.

## On the computer and on the site

- **A sound from a prompt.** Any language model (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a local one) designs a
  LAB sound from a few words with SIDOU's [instructions](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/prompt-to-patch.md)
  (served as [llms.txt](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/llms.txt)): it answers with a JSON patch
  in musical units ([the format](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/reference/patch-json.md))
  and a patch link. The site's [Library](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html) page checks
  it with the FM-1's own rules (each refusal one line naming the field, to paste back), plays it,
  and sends it as a one-instrument song LAB opens with ENV; no sign-in, nothing stored. Any of the
  sixteen sounds, or a LAB sound in a song, shows as a patch to change; several at once come as a
  bank of up to sixteen (`sidou-bank/1`), sent as one song; and a STEP pattern (`sidou-step/1`: a
  bassline or a beat with slides, accents and chances) comes as a song that opens on STEP and
  plays. The site's sixteen sounds are templates: their quality depends on the model.
- **`sidou-sysex` for coding agents.** `sidou-sysex patch|bank|step FILE.json --out DIR` writes the
  song and its `.syx` with the same converter the site uses, and `sidou-sysex send FILE` (macOS)
  uploads a song, tune or instrument to the FM-1 after checking that the port answers as SIDOU,
  never a firmware package ([docs/ai](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/ai/README.md)).
- **Five of the site's sixteen sounds filter every voice.** PWM STRINGS, 303 BASS, WOBBLE BASS,
  S&H BLIPS and SWEEP PAD filtered voice 1 only, so the notes LAB played on voices 2 and 3 were
  unfiltered; a patch can no longer do that on a one-oscillator sound.
- **A patch is refused as silent only when it is.** The page and `sidou-sysex` play it through
  the song player on both chips instead of estimating, so a classic acid bass (low cutoff, high
  resonance, the filter envelope doing the work) is taken.

## Measured

The image is 548,320 bytes, 31,196 under its ceiling of 579,516 (the app slot less 2 KB; the
ceiling was 568,000 in 1.7, and 1.8's felucca-bsp move, 44.1 kHz recording and smaller cold files
made room for Bluetooth). The SRAM for the audio path is 44,832 of 49,152 bytes used. The site's
wasm: `sidou-core.wasm` 165,106 bytes of 176 KiB, the patch checker `sidou-patch.wasm` 71,737
bytes of 80 KiB, loaded only when a patch is checked.

**Bluetooth MIDI** is tested but not shipped in this release. The test build (`SIDOU_BLE=ON`)
takes 579,968 bytes, 452 over the release ceiling of 579,516: before
it ships in a release it must fit 579,516 again, through felucca-bsp#31 (the radio driver at plain
`-Os`, about 3.2 KB) or another saving. Three device checks are still open: an hour of two SIDs
with Bluetooth MIDI in from a Mac, a save during the link (the store's erase holds up to 35 ms),
and the DAW clock.

**Tested on a real FM-1** (2026-11-07): the live `.sid` tweaks and the ACID role were tested in
fm1-emu only, not by ear on a device; the felucca-bsp items (the install over 1.7, back-to-back
SysEx, USB-MIDI both ways and USB audio at 44.1 kHz, and ten power-ons) were checked on a real
FM-1 on 2026-11-07 (hardware-test.md section 34); `sidou-sysex send` and a patch played in LAB
were checked on a real FM-1 running 1.7.0. The 1.8.0 package itself: an install over the Bluetooth test build, a boot, songs and settings kept, and a save kept over a power-cycle.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.8.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.

**sidou-1.8.0.fwsc**, identity FM-1_964, SHA-256 `26195671c5ce6db8d2f370210e9116e728dbdf184115f9313ddfc450342a4734`.
