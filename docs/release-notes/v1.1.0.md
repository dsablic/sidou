# SIDOU 1.1.0: hear it before you keep it

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html) ("What's new in 1.1" is new).

## New

- **LAB grows.** FILTER's K4 is a filter LFO (the cutoff sweeps at the PWM's rate, the drawing
  shows the band); MOD's K4 is the arpeggio's RATE; a sixth page, MACRO, links the resonance to
  the cutoff and the PWM's depth to the pulse width, on LIVE's knobs and over MIDI too; and the
  keys played while EDIT is held sound the sound as kept (A), to compare as you play. Sounds kept
  in 1.0 load as they were.
- **Hear before you write.** TABLE's ARP helper plays its draft on the keys (`HEARING DRAFT`),
  and a held STEP step sounds with its cutoff, resonance, filter, route and pulse locks.
- **Generative TRACKER.** EDIT + ARP is MUTATE and EDIT + SCL is DICE in TRACKER too; DICE has a
  range and a rhythm (K2, K4), a root from a note key and a scale for each lane; EDIT + ENV
  writes a filter sweep. All of it is plain GoatTracker data. A song with 1.1's DICE settings
  still loads in 1.0.
- **Knob curves.** The PWM's rate and depth, the vibrato's rate, depth and delay, and every effect
  slot's knobs step evenly to the ear. Stored values keep their meaning.
- **Songs to MIDI reads from the FM-1:** the site lists the songs on a connected FM-1 and
  converts one, wavetable arpeggios included.

## Changed

- **REC from the keys lands where you played** (6 ms earlier than the key is read).
- **An upload waits for quiet** while a song plays or a computer records the USB audio, so the
  music never dips; with only the keys sounding, writes are 250 ms apart. The Songs page shows
  the wait. An older SysEx tool may time out while a song plays: stop the song first.
- **The vibrato's CCs (76-78) and the effect slots' CCs (102-116) land on the knob curves'
  positions.** DAW automation of them recorded with 1.0 can sound different; their NRPNs still
  set exact values.

## Fixed

- **CLOCK OUT:** PLAY after a stop sends a start, so a synced DAW restarts with SIDOU, and a
  start, stop or continue is no longer lost while a SysEx reply is going out.
- **EDIT chords work whichever finger lands first** in LAB, STEP and TRACKER.
- **The Songs page waits and resends** when the FM-1 answers BUSY.
- **The settings are saved** after two failed flash writes in a row.
- **The Distortion slot is heard:** TAPE saturates, OVERDRIVE and FUZZ clip instead of only
  getting louder, CRUSH's top is a 4-bit buzz. Saved sounds with a distortion sound more driven
  than in 1.0. EDIT + K1-K4 on LIVE show the effect, the control and its value in a large popup.

**Tested in the emulator, not yet on a real FM-1** (reports welcome): an upload from the Songs
page while a song plays (it waits for quiet), CLOCK OUT with a synced DAW after a stop, and
Songs to MIDI reading from a connected FM-1.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.1.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.
