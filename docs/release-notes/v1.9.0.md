# SIDOU 1.9.0: Bluetooth MIDI

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html), with a new *What's new in 1.9*.

## On the FM-1

- **Bluetooth MIDI.** SIDOU is a Bluetooth MIDI device a Mac, an iPad or a phone connects to,
  with no pairing: it shows up as `SIDOU XXXX`. Notes, CCs and a DAW's clock come in, and the
  panel's CCs and program changes go out, as over USB. A dropped link lets go of the held notes.
  Turn it on with HOME, SETTINGS, BLUETOOTH (the last item), ALGORITHM. It is **OFF by default**,
  and the setting and SIDOU's Bluetooth address are kept. With it off, the radio never starts
  and SIDOU runs as it did without Bluetooth.
- **One package for everyone.** Bluetooth is in the normal package; there is no separate build.
  Every boot now sets the FM-1's stock power-up (the rails and the DC-DC, M-VAVE's own values),
  and STATUS's READY line shows its record.
- **USB audio follows the FM-1's own clock.** The packets carry the I2S clock's rate, 44 frames a
  millisecond and one more every 8 or 9, so a host that follows the device closely (iOS's Camera)
  no longer hears a dropout. What a DAW records is the same, sample for sample.
- Sounds, songs and settings from 1.8 load unchanged.

## On the computer and on the site

- *What's new in 1.9*, and every video re-rendered on 1.9.

## Measured

The image is 577,136 bytes, 2,380 under its ceiling of 579,516 (the app slot less 2 KB). The
radio driver builds in its own unit at `-Os` (felucca-bsp#31), 2,992 bytes smaller.
The SRAM for the audio path is 44,864 of 49,152 bytes used. With BLUETOOTH OFF the main loop
costs 2.40M instructions a second idle, as in 1.8.

**Tested on a real FM-1** (2026-11-08, hardware-test.md section 35), on the Bluetooth build this
release's radio code comes from: BLUETOOTH ON and OFF, the Mac connecting before and after a power
cycle, notes from the Mac and CC 74 to it, a dropped link releasing a held note, two minutes of
chords with no click, a firmware install with BLUETOOTH ON, an hour of two SIDs with Bluetooth
MIDI in (1,796 bars, no reboot, the link up throughout), and saves during the link. The 1.9.0
package itself: an install over that build, a boot with the settings kept, two minutes of USB audio
recorded with no gap or click, and SIDOU following a DAW's clock and transport over Bluetooth.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.9.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.

**sidou-1.9.0.fwsc**, identity FM-1_964, SHA-256 `f88bbead3178fcd3894a67f45d262d2009c812eb32e87ed3474711887390d445`.
