# SIDOU 1.6.0: layered sounds, a filter envelope, and SidStation patches

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html).

## On the FM-1

- **Layers in LAB.** A sound plays up to three oscillators on one key, each with its own
  waveform, envelope, pulse, transpose and detune, on LAB's new LAYERS page. ALGORITHM sets how
  many; OSC and ENVELOPE edit the layer K1 picks (their header says `L2`). A layered note takes
  the chip's three voices. Sounds kept in 1.5 load unchanged, as one layer.
- **FILTER ENV in LAB.** The filter has an envelope of its own: depth on ALGORITHM, attack,
  decay, sustain and release on the cards, drawn as it plays.
- **No title cards.** CHIP and SCENE open at once, and HOME from the menu goes straight back to
  LIVE. SETTINGS loses its CARDS row; the boot's title screen stays.

## On the computer

- **`sidou-sysex import`** turns an Elektron SidStation or MIDIbox SID V2 bank into songs of
  sixteen patches. Every instrument keeps its layered LAB sound: load the song, pick the
  instrument in INSTR and press ENV to play it in LAB, SAVE to keep it. A SidStation step table
  plays as a GoatTracker wave program, and a report says what each patch keeps
  ([the guide](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/manual/import.md)).

## Measured

SIDOU's 6581 filter was measured against recordings of real chips: it falls inside the spread
between real chips, so a heavier filter model would not be heard.

**Read as best known, worth reporting:** two SidStation details are undocumented: a table's
fixed notes are taken as MIDI note numbers and its speed as a divider of the patch's update rate.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.6.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.
