# SIDOU 1.20.0: Bluetooth keyboards, RED MOON RITE's art as a file, a refreshed SCENE

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html), with a new *What's new in 1.20*.

## On the FM-1

- **Bluetooth keyboards.** SETTINGS' BLUETOOTH is now OFF, ON or KEYBOARD. With KEYBOARD, SAVE
  opens DEVICES, the Bluetooth MIDI keyboards nearby with signal bars; SAVE on one connects it.
  A keyboard that asks to pair is paired (nothing to confirm, or a passkey SIDOU shows large for
  you to type), the bond is kept, and SIDOU connects to it again after a power cycle or when it
  comes back in range. Its notes, knobs and clock play as from USB; toasts say when it connects
  and when it is lost (held notes stop). One link at a time: with KEYBOARD a computer cannot
  connect, and ON lets it back. Tested with a Korg microKEY Air.
- **RED MOON RITE's art is a file on the FM-1.** The Library page's **RED MOON RITE art** button
  (or `sidou-sysex art`) sends the pictures of SCENE's barbarian duel, an art pack of 41 KB the
  FM-1 keeps in its store. Until it arrives, RED MOON RITE plays SIDOU's logo intro, the logo
  between drifting starfields glowing in the colour of what you play, with the hint
  `RED MOON RITE ART: LIBRARY`. FORMAT erases the pack with the rest; a backup keeps it.
- **SCENE refreshed.** It opens on **DEMO**, the logo intro as a piece of its own, then FIRE,
  PLASMA, **SCOPE** (the three voices of the chip the keys play as glowing oscilloscope traces)
  and RED MOON RITE, which now **fights to anything played**: the keys, MIDI, Bluetooth and
  songs without SIDOU's drum kit play it by their notes (high notes chop overhead, low ones
  sweep low, a chord flares the torches, a fast run is a flurry). RULE 30, STARS and ANT are gone.
- **The mod wheel adds vibrato** on every sound. The MIDI page's new **WHEEL** row picks what it
  moves: VIBRATO, PULSE or CUTOFF. On PULSE it stays in an audible range.
- **LAB's card glows** in its knob's colour for the second it is lit.

## On the site

- **Your own RED MOON RITE:** the [art pack builder](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/art.html)
  takes a PNG per slot (the fighters, the swords, the skies, the arena, the status bar), reduces
  it to the screen's 16 colours, plays the duel with the firmware's own code, and downloads the
  `.sidart` or sends it to the FM-1.

## Under the hood

felucca-bsp `18eadadb` (USB audio packets sized by the FM-1's crystal alone); cold code built at
`-Oz`; `-DSIDOU_CPU2=ON`, a build option off by default that renders chip B on the second core,
for a later device check; CI on every push.

## Measured

The image is 548,228 bytes, 31,288 under its ceiling of 579,516 (RED MOON RITE's art moved to the
store). RAMTEXT holds 44,864 bytes of its 48 KB. The site's patch module is 83,921 bytes of its
88 KiB.

**Tested on a real FM-1** (2026-11-15 and 16, hardware-test.md sections 38 to 40), with this package for section 40: an install over the test build with the songs, the keyboard and its bond and the art pack kept; 1.20.0 on ABOUT; a Korg microKEY Air reconnecting without pairing, its mod wheel adding vibrato; SCENE opening on DEMO, RED MOON RITE's duel played from the keys and a song; BLUETOOTH OFF and ON, and ON kept over a power cycle. Earlier test builds passed pairing, LOST and reconnecting, the bond over a power cycle, a Mac connecting over Bluetooth with notes, CCs and the clock, an install with BLUETOOTH ON, the art pack sent from the Library, and a broken pack refused.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.20.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Your songs and settings are kept. Then send
RED MOON RITE's art from the [Library page](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html). Back to
stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc` the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.
