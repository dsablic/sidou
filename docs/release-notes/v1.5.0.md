# SIDOU 1.5.0: from the sound to the song, and onto the computer

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html) ("What's new" is new).

## On the FM-1

- **LAB's song bridge.** REC in LAB sends the sound to the song as a GoatTracker instrument while
  the song plays (`SENT AS I06 SAW LEAD`); what had to be approximated is said on the hint line.
  On INSTR, ENV, LFO or FX pulls an instrument back into LAB; one you sent and didn't edit comes
  back exactly.
- **Lift a sound from a `.sid`.** In NOW PLAYING, solo a voice and press REC: its next note is
  captured as a GoatTracker instrument, offered to your song when the tune ends.
- **Chords and drums from DICE.** Set a lane's role in DICE's frame (EDIT + SCL held, PRESETS): an
  ARP lane rolls chords, a BASS+DRUMS lane puts the kit's kick and snare on the strong beats with
  the bass between, as C64 musicians share one voice.
- **Less squinting and scrolling.** Every knob that changes something shown in small print shows
  its name and value large; EDIT + SELECT jumps a page in ORDER, TABLE and INSTR; INSTR says what
  each table pointer points to (`PUL +0 +4 +7`, `50% PWM`) and the envelope in milliseconds.

## On the computer

- **Live Link:** the FM-1's screen in your browser, its buttons and knobs on screen, and the
  computer keyboard as a tracker keyboard (notes on two rows, arrows, page jumps, Shift for EDIT).
- **The site's library:** list, download, send, rename, delete, back up and restore what is on the
  FM-1.
- **The site's song editor:** instruments, tables, patterns and orderlists with a computer
  keyboard, preview with SIDOU's own player, save to the FM-1 or download.
- **MIDI controller maps** for shaping sounds from an eight-knob controller
  ([controllers](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/manual/controllers.md)).

## Fixed

- Two value popups named the wrong control (EDIT + a knob with ARP held on LIVE; two knobs on
  ORDER that change nothing).
- A DICE setting turned while a roll was still writing said `SONG FULL`; it says `WAITING`.

The firmware is also 8.5 KB smaller, with nothing heard or seen changed.

**Tested in the emulator, not yet on a real FM-1** (reports welcome): Live Link and the site's
library and editor over real Web MIDI.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.5.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.
