# SIDOU 1.7.0: step tables, free LFOs, slide and accent

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html), with a new *What's new in 1.7*.

## On the FM-1

- **TABLE in LAB.** A sound carries a step table, as a SidStation or a C64 tune does: up to 16
  steps, each a waveform and a pitch (from the key, or a fixed note) for so many frames, played
  once or looped from a step, on every note. One key plays a noise attack into a pulse, an octave
  arpeggio or a drum that drops in pitch. REC sends it to the song as the instrument's GoatTracker
  wave program, and ENV on INSTR reads a wave program back as a table.
- **Two free LFOs in LAB.** MOD's second page (LFO on MOD goes there): triangle, saw up, saw down,
  square or sample and hold, on the pitch, the pulse width, the cutoff or the chip's volume,
  0.05 to 12.75 Hz, drawn side by side. LAB has ten pages.
- **SLIDE and ACCENT in STEP.** Hold a step and press FX: the note slides into the next one (its
  gate held, a 4-frame glide). Hold a step and press ENV: the step is accented (cutoff, resonance
  and sustain up for its row). Both are locks, saved with the song.
- **An NRPN for every LAB card.** FILTER ENV, LAYERS, TABLE and LFO's cards are NRPN group 7, so a
  DAW can automate each (receive only; [the MIDI map](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/reference/midi.md)).
- Sounds kept in 1.6 load unchanged, with no table and the LFOs off; a 1.6 SIDOU plays a 1.7
  sound without its table and LFOs.

## On the computer and on the site

- **SIDOU's own sounds.** The site's [Library](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html) page
  sends sixteen original LAB sounds to the FM-1 as one song, `SIDOU SOUNDS` (a table kick and
  snare, a chip chord, wobble and acid basses, a 303 BASS for slides and accents, LFO strings,
  layered saws and more): ENV on INSTR opens each in LAB.
- **`sidou-sysex import` keeps a SidStation patch's table and LFOs.** The first playing
  oscillator's step table becomes the LAB sound's (the others still play from their song
  instruments, and the report names them), and the vibrato, PWM and filter LFOs map onto LAB's
  two. An imported table no longer plays each step a frame too long.

## Measured

GoatTracker's player now runs from flash, as it is called once a song frame, not once a sample:
6.4 KB of the SRAM kept for the audio path is free again (42,752 of 49,152 bytes used), room for
1.8's live tweaks on a playing `.sid` and for chip B on the second core. The image is 552,384
bytes of 568,000. fm1-emu has no instruction cache, so the cost of the player's cache misses on a
real FM-1 is estimated, at up to about 4 % of an audio block once a song frame; the device test
below measures it.

Three things are not proven yet and are worth reporting:

- The SidStation's LFO format is undocumented: the import's mapping of its shapes, speeds and
  depths is a best guess. A patch whose vibrato or sweep runs at a clearly wrong speed or depth is
  worth an issue.
- The sixteen sounds on the site were designed by numbers and checked by a render and SIDOU's
  audibility estimate; none has been ear-checked on a real FM-1 yet.
- **A song with a slide or an accent loses all its locks in SIDOU 1.6.** Slide and accent ride
  the song's lock chunk, and 1.6 drops a lock chunk with a lock it does not know: such a song plays
  in 1.6 without any of its locks (cutoff, resonance, chance and the rest). It plays whole again
  in 1.7.

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-1.7.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's `FM-1.fwsc`
the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.

**sidou-1.7.0.fwsc**, identity FM-1_964, SHA-256 `SHA-PENDING`.
