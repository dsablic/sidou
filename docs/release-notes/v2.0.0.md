# SIDOU 2.0.0: the second core, PUNCH, and MULTI and WHOLE A for a DAW

**Try it in your browser first:** <https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/>, the
[cheat sheet](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/cheatsheet.html) and the
[how-to videos](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/videos.html), with a new *What's new in 2.0*.

## On the FM-1

- **Chip B on the second core.** The FM-1's second core renders chip B, so the first core's audio
  time is about halved (SIDOU LOOP with the keys on the owner's FM-1: on average 31.4 % to 16.6 %
  of a block, at worst 41.7 % to 29.4 %). A self-test at start-up checks core 1 first; a unit
  whose core 1 fails it, misreads, stops answering or faults plays on one core with the same
  sound until power-off. STATUS's new fourth page, CORES, shows core 1 running or why it is off,
  its jobs, where chip B renders and core 0's audio time; ALGORITHM there moves chip B between the
  cores to compare.
- **PUNCH.** FX on any song view opens a page whose first six note keys are held effect pads for
  the playing song, several at once: MUTE, FILTER SLAM, PITCH DIVE (a tape stop, an octave down in
  half a second), STUTTER (the beat repeats; let go, the song is exactly where it would have
  been), ECHO THROW (sent into a delay whose echoes ring on) and CHIP SWAP (the song on the other
  SID model). Each is undone when its key is let go, and nothing is written into the song.
- **MULTI plays four parts from a DAW.** Channels N, N+1 and N+2 are a monophonic part each on
  chip A's three voices, each with its own LAB sound (program change 0..3 the presets, 4 the
  instrument's sound, 16..31 USER 1..16), velocity, bend, mod wheel and aftertouch; N+3 plays the
  instrument on chip B. The MIDI page shows and picks each channel's sound.
- **WHOLE A plays one LAB sound on chip A's three voices:** POLY (three notes, the oldest giving
  way), CHORD (a chord from one key) or ARP (the held notes stepped at a rate or on the MIDI
  clock). Program change or the MIDI page's `CH` row picks the sound.
- **A layer's level in LAB.** LAYERS' K4 is LEVEL, 0 to 100 %, the page showing each layer's
  level as a bar. It is kept in the sound, the USER sounds and `sidou-patch/1`; an older sound
  reads at full level. Which layers the filter takes is FILTER's K3 VOICES.
- **Aftertouch adds vibrato** on every sound by default, as the mod wheel does.
- **Fixed:** a second knob while a popup shows no longer sends a frame over 200 cells; a text
  song's first row plays at its tempo.

A new manual page, [SIDOU from a DAW](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/docs/manual/daw.md),
sets a sequencer up for each MIDI mode, the clock, USB audio recording and Bluetooth.

## On the site

- **Sounds from a `.sid` tune.** After the Library page converts a tune, the instruments it found
  are listed as LAB sounds, with their voice, the notes that use them and what LAB could not hold.
  Listen, keep (16 in the browser, all as a bank), edit as text, send to the FM-1 or copy a link.
  Nothing is uploaded, and the original's copyright applies to the sounds.

## Measured

The image is 567,540 bytes, 11,976 under its ceiling of 579,516. RAMTEXT holds 45,920 bytes of
its 48 KB. The site's core is 167,274 bytes of its 176 KiB, the patch module 85,842 of its 88 KiB,
the tune module 78,646 of its 96 KiB.

**Tested on a real FM-1** (2026-11-18, hardware-test.md sections 42 and 43): the second core (an
FPU on core 1, 30 minutes of chip B on it with no misread, core 0's audio time measured, a
restart), a layer's level, aftertouch's vibrato, MULTI's four parts, PUNCH's pads and WHOLE A's
POLY, CHORD and ARP, measured from the FM-1's USB audio; the release package itself:
2026-11-18, hardware-test.md sections 42 to 44: chip B on core 1 through a 30-minute soak, its audio time on core 0 halved; a layer's level, aftertouch, MULTI and WHOLE A measured over USB; PUNCH, the LAYERS card and the keyboard's reconnection by the owner; then this package installed over the tested build, 2.0.0 on ABOUT.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Over USB, at your own risk; the official firmware is one install away. Use Felucca's installer:
`fm1_install.py sidou-2.0.0.fwsc`, from the stock firmware, a Felucca-family firmware or an
earlier SIDOU (it finds the FM-1 as "SIDOU FM-1"). Your songs and settings are kept. Coming from
before 1.20, send RED MOON RITE's art from the
[Library page](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html). Back to stock: install M-VAVE's
`FM-1.fwsc` the same way. Holding OCT- at power-on is a rescue.
