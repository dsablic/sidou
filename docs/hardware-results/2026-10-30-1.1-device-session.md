# 1.1 on a real FM-1, 2026-10-30

The first 1.1.0 candidate (`333f9d79…`, `tools/preflash` PASS) installed over 1.0.0 in about 70 s.

## With the user

- LAB: the filter LFO (FILTER's K4), the MACRO links, the peek, MOD's ARP RATE: work, "sounds ok"
  / "sounds good".
- A held STEP step plays its locks; DICE's range and rhythm, TRACKER's MUTATE, DICE and sweep:
  work.
- The knob curves of the PWM and the vibrato: work. REC from the keys lands on the beat.
- **The ARP helper restarted the FM-1** when opened on an empty WAVE row (the built-in loop,
  stopped): the audition's draft was copied with 32-bit stores to an odd address, which the chip
  faults on and fm1-emu does not model. Fixed (the draft aligned, a static assert, and an
  alignment check in the emulator tests that fails without the fix).
- **The effect knobs (EDIT + K1-K4) did little:** the Distortion slot's algorithms got about
  12 dB less than they are made for (1.0 the same). Fixed (the slot lifts its input x4), and the
  large popup with the effect and its value added, at the user's request.

The second candidate (`31b73ec1…`, PASS) with both fixes: the ARP helper opens without a restart,
the distortions and the popup "all works".
