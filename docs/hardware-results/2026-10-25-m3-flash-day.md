# M3 (.sid playback) on a real FM-1, 2026-10-25

[hardware-test.md](../hardware-test.md) section 21, with the user. The M3 build (`m3-base`
a3640cf1, FM-1_964, SHA-256 `550c4ac4…`, `tools/preflash` PASS) installed over v0.4.0 in about
66 s; then the STATUS change below (1442e3cf, `9160d82e…`) the same way.

## The tunes on the store

The starter pack (11 tunes) and the study's four heaviest HVSC tunes went in over USB SysEx `PUT`
type 2 with `sidou-flash/tools/sysex_put_file.py` (stop-and-wait on each ACK). The Last Ninja
(35,617 bytes) was refused part way with `NAK BUSY` (the store's pool short, the refusal section
20 expects) and went in whole when sent again 20 s later; the sender gives up on BUSY instead of
waiting and retrying (https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/v0.5.0/docs/TODO.md). DIR listed all 15.

## The 6502's share of the CPU (STATUS `CPU`)

| Tune | Device | fm1-emu | LATE on the device | LATE in fm1-emu, 60 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Last Ninja | 4.0-4.2 % | 1.6-1.8 % | not read | about 5,200 |
| Kill Me | about 5 % | 2.0 % | 120-200 after 30-40 s | 88 (from 30.8 s) |
| Thomkat with Strange End | about 8 % | 3.3-3.5 % | 120-200 after 30-40 s | 385 |
| Sound Test | 10-12 % | 4.6-5.2 % | not read | 260,550 (STATUS shows 9999) |

No overruns; nothing heard amiss. The device reads 2.3-2.5 times fm1-emu's figure on every tune
(the flash fetches fm1-emu does not model). The worst, Sound Test, is under the rule's 15 %.

## Late writes: a capacity limit, not the device's speed

A block renders in at most `PSID_SPLITS - 1` = 15 parts, one a distinct write position; later
writes in the block land at its end (at most 2.9 ms after their cycle, in order, nothing dropped).
fm1-emu counts the same with the 6502 two frames ahead, and a host run of `psid_ctl` the same, so
the rule's "a late write fm1-emu does not have" is not met and the CPU fallbacks would not change
them. Kill Me's device count may be a little above fm1-emu's at 60 s; it was read as a range, so
no device lag is shown. Raising `PSID_SPLITS` to 24 (about 3-6 interrupt instructions a sample,
no RAM) clears all but Sound Test: planned for 0.5.x (https://github.com/dsablic/sidou/blob/v0.5.0/docs/TODO.md).

Follow-up (2026-10-28): `PSID_SPLITS` is 24. In fm1-emu the late writes of Thomkat, Kill Me and
The Last Ninja drop to 0; `PSID_BLOCK_ENTRIES` went 100 to 80 (at 24 renders and 100 entries the 4-cycle bench's
block cost 123.1 of `PSID_ISR_WORST` 120, now 94.9; a new bench tune of one write on every sample
is 87.2); RAMTEXT is unchanged (48,736 bytes). Sound
Test still needs 61 parts. A device re-check of LATE is the open step.

## Decision

At or under 15 % with no late write of the device's own: **M3 ships as built** (the user agreed).

## The screen

The user found STATUS's 1× text too small on the device; it became 2× on three pages that SELECT
turns (ba56e593), installed and approved ("looks good"). The three UI choices were approved as
built: MIDI Stop pauses a tune; SIDS after SONG; NOW PLAYING's envelope bars.

## Not checked on the device

The song views' `SID PLAYING: STOP TO EDIT` while a tune plays, PLAY's stop and `--` with no
tune, a 2SID tune, and the unit's other clock (not needed: under the rule). fm1-emu covers the
first two (`psid_views.rs`).
