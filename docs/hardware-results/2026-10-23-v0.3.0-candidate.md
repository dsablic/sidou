# v0.3.0 candidate on a real FM-1, 2026-10-23

## The first candidate: an update blocker

`sidou-0.3.0.fwsc` (main 67cff685 + the version bump, FM-1_964, SHA-256 `56c76cc4…`) installed
over the previous build and ran normally (handshake 10/10, both inquiries, SysEx 4 KB and 64 KB
byte-identical), but **as the running firmware it could not serve an update**: every install,
of itself or of an older package, ended "the FM-1 was disconnected after 4 requests; nothing was
written" and the unit restarted into the same build. Cause (bisected in fm1-emu to 30d1eb9b,
review 4.5): EP1 NAKs the host while SIDOU's MIDI-in ring is nearly full, and nothing empties the
ring during an update session, so the installer was held off after two replies and step 1 failed.
Fixed in bdc73089 (the session's idle empties the ring and releases EP1), with an emulator test
that runs a whole step 1 through the running firmware. The unit was recovered with the OCT-
gesture into ROM boot and the unbricker's `restore` of the fixed package.
