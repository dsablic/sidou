# A LAB sound as text: `sidou-patch/1`

One LAB sound as JSON, with named fields in musical units: a wave by name, the cutoff in Hz, the
envelope as the 0..15 steps LAB's cards show. It is what a language model writes from
[the instructions](../prompt-to-patch.md), what the site's [Library](https://dsablic.github.io/sidou/library.html)
reads, checks, previews and sends to the FM-1, and what it shows any of SIDOU's sixteen sounds as.
The JSON Schema is [patch.schema.json](patch.schema.json); the site serves both as
`llms.txt` and `patch.schema.json` at its root, and the sixteen as `sounds/patches/*.json`.

```json
{
  "format": "sidou-patch/1",
  "name": "303 BASS",
  "wave": "saw",
  "envelope": {"attack": 0, "decay": 8, "sustain": 8, "release": 8},
  "filter": {"cutoff_hz": 2077, "resonance": 12, "voices": [1], "envelope": {"depth": 40, "decay": 6, "sustain": 0}},
  "vibrato": {"rate_hz": 0}
}
```

A patch holds everything a LAB sound holds ([sound-format.md](sound-format.md), the 109 bytes of
`app/lab_sound.h`) and its name; not the chip, its character or the octave, which are the
instrument's. One converter, `platform/web/patch_json.c`, reads a patch into the 109 bytes and
writes the 109 bytes as a patch: the site runs it as WebAssembly (`tools/wasm-patch`), the host
tests through `sidou-sys`. A patch written from a sound reads back as exactly that sound.

## Reading

- `format` is required and is `"sidou-patch/1"`. Every other field may be left out.
- What is left out is LAB's own starting sound, SAW LEAD as the FM-1 loads it at power-on.
  A layer is a copy of the top-level oscillator but what it names; a table step plays its layer's
  wave unless it names one; an effect takes its type's settings (the FM-1's own, as its CHIP view
  picks a type) but what it names; an LFO is a triangle unless it names a shape. `null` is left out.
- The length of `layers` is the number of extra oscillators, of `table.steps` the table's steps.
  A table with steps is on unless `"on": false`.
- `name` is upper-cased; it is the instrument's name in the song and LAB's name for the sound.
- Text before the first `{` and after the patch is skipped, so a model's whole answer can be
  pasted.
- A number takes up to three decimals; `cutoff_hz`, the LFOs' `rate_hz` and the percentages
  become the nearest value the sound can hold.
- A patch is refused, with the field, what is wrong and what is allowed, when a field is unknown,
  of the wrong type or out of its range; when `layers` meet `sync_lead`; when a table is on with no
  steps or loops past its last step; and when LAB's audibility estimate (the one its rolls use,
  `lab_audible`) hears nothing on either chip. Silent on one chip only, it is taken with a warning.

## Writing

A sound is written with `format`, `name`, the `wave` and the `envelope`, then every field whose
value is not what reading would fill in. Numbers are the shortest decimal that reads back as the
same value.

## Banks: `sidou-bank/1`

Up to sixteen patches as one song, an instrument each, the way the site's sixteen sounds go
(the sixteen as a bank, `assets/sounds/sidou-sounds-bank.json`, make `sidou-sounds.sng` byte for
byte). Its schema is [bank.schema.json](bank.schema.json).

```json
{"format": "sidou-bank/1", "name": "ACID KIT", "patches": [{"name": "KICK", "...": "..."}, {"name": "BASS"}]}
```

- `format` is required; `name` is the song's name on the FM-1, 1 to 16 characters (a store name:
  the font's upper case, not starting with a space), `BANK` when left out; `patches` holds 1 to 16
  patches, each read as above, its `format` optional.
- A refusal names the patch: `patches[1].wave: "sawtooth" is not allowed. ...`. A bank whose
  sounds' tables do not fit one song's 255 rows each is refused at the patch that overflows.
- Its link is `library.html#bank=`, made as a patch's; the sixteen are a 6.8 KB link, inside the
  page's 8 KB.

## The link

`library.html#patch=` followed by the patch's JSON, percent-encoded (what `encodeURIComponent`
writes), opens the patch on the Library page: it checks and previews it and shows Send to FM-1, and
sends nothing by itself. A fragment never reaches a server. The longest of the sixteen sounds is a
link of under 2 KB.
