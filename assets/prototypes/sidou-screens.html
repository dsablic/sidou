SIDOU Screens

A working prototype of the FM-1's 240×240 screen. Every frame is a 30×30 grid of 8×8 cells in the 16-colour palette, updated 25 times a second from a small three-voice pattern. Nothing here is a picture: the font, frames, scenes and logos are all drawn in code.

FM-1 · SIDOU 0.1
 

Click the screen to focus it. MENU: ↑ ↓ and Return (or click a line twice). SCENE: ← → or click. STEP and TRACKER: 1–3 mute a voice. Any key or click skips the boot and leaves ABOUT.

STEP, arriving in M2 with songs. The 16 keys are the 16 steps of the page; hold a step and turn a knob to lock a parameter on it (K1 cutoff, K2 pulse width, K3 wave, K4 decay). Locked steps carry a corner notch. The encoder pages through the 64-step pattern (← → here), and the held step follows the pattern; click a step, a key, or a lane name to hold it, or mute that lane. Chip B gets its own lane once the second chip plays.

TRACKER, arriving in M2 with songs. A GoatTracker 2 style grid, 64 rows in hex. Each voice is packed into 8 cells, C-401308: note (white), instrument (light blue), then command and parameter (yellow, green), so 3 08 is tone portamento and 4 22 vibrato. === is gate off, dots are empty. Click a column head to mute it; Return toggles between GT2 scrolling and a moving bar over still rows, which costs far fewer tiles.

Cutoff drives the plasma speed and the filter curve. Gates step the automaton and the ants, envelopes heat the fire, the kick warps the stars. Muting a voice stops its part of every scene.

Rendering cost on the device

The LCD takes about 77 ms to redraw all 240×240 pixels over SPI, so redrawing everything would cap the screen near 13 fps. SIDOU sends only the 8×8 tiles that changed. Every screen and piece below runs in the background on the same pattern at 25 fps; a tile counts when any of its 64 pixels differs from the previous frame.

Typical is the mean over the last 5 s, peak the maximum. SPI time = tiles × 0.086 ms (77 ms ÷ 900 tiles) + 0.02 ms per horizontal strip for the window command. The tick in each bar marks 20 ms, half a frame, as the budget.

Logo explorations

Four original marks, each drawn in code from the 16 colours. Left: on the device at 1:1, inside the boot title card. Right: 4× for detail. The one you pick is used on the BOOT and ABOUT screens.

Notes for the firmware

  • The font is an original 8×8 face with chamfered corners and 2-pixel stems. The graphics glyphs (quadrants, eighth-fills, Bayer dithers, rounded frame corners) are generated, so the C version can be a table built the same way.
  • Fire and plasma change most of the screen and need a frame-rate cap or a smaller window on the device. Rule 30, ants, stars, menus and the LIVE view fit comfortably.
  • The boot's loading stripes repaint the whole border every frame (224 tiles); keep that phase short or update the stripes every other frame.
  • Pieces are inspired by the catalogue at ascii.rest; all code here is new.
