Screen readability at real size

The FM-1's screen packs 240 pixels into about 23 mm, so SIDOU's 8-pixel font comes out at 0.8 mm. Each pair below shows a screen as it is today next to a proposal. Both are drawn with SIDOU's own font and 16-colour palette, at the size the device shows them, with a 1:1 pixel view beside them. Calibrate first, so "real size" is real on your monitor.

1 · Calibrate your screen

Hold a bank card (85.60 mm wide) against the bar and drag the slider until they match. Then measure the FM-1's lit screen area with a ruler and enter its width.

The proposals use only what the device can draw: the 16-colour palette and SIDOU's 8×8 font, scaled 2× or 3× by doubling pixels. That is the method the large-text work in progress already uses. Black backgrounds, one colour per voice and knob, and at least 16-pixel text for anything you read while playing.
