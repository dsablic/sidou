Everything in your FM-1's store, from the browser: the songs, the .sid tunes and the instruments. Download them, send your own, rename, delete, and back the whole store up to one .zip and restore it. And any song, from the FM-1 or a file, exported for GoatTracker 2 and its instruments as .ins files, by SIDOU's own song code running in the page: what it accepts and refuses is what the FM-1 accepts and refuses.

This browser cannot reach the FM-1. That needs Web MIDI with SysEx, which Chrome and Edge have. Safari has no Web MIDI, and Firefox is not supported by this page. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Chrome on Android also works with a USB cable). Exporting a song from a file works in any browser.

1. Connect

Plug in the FM-1 (running SIDOU, not in the updater) and press Connect. The browser asks to use your MIDI devices with SysEx: allow it. This page sends nothing until you press Connect, and then only to the pair you see selected below.

2. On the FM-1

NameTypeSizeCRC-32

A file sent under a name the FM-1 already has is replaced only after you confirm; an identical one is left alone. Rename reads the file, sends it under the new name and deletes the old one only once the new one is stored. A backup holds every file listed here, with a sidou-backup.json that keeps their names and types; restoring it sends back what is missing or different (asking first) and deletes nothing. The FM-1's private files (a song or instrument it is importing, and the copy it keeps until you SAVE AS) are never listed, so they are never read, backed up or touched. While a song plays on the FM-1 (or a computer records its USB audio) it writes nothing, so the music never dips: the page says it is waiting for the FM-1 to be quiet until you stop the song.

3. SIDOU's sounds

Sixteen LAB sounds made with SIDOU's own pages (the table, the LFOs, the layers, the filter envelope), in one song. Send it, then on the FM-1: menu, SONG, LOAD SIDOU SOUNDS, SEQ to INSTR, PRESETS to a sound and ENV: it opens in LAB as it was made, and SAVE keeps it as one of your USER sounds.

    4. A sound from a prompt

    Ask any language model for a sound: copy SIDOU's instructions into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a model on your computer (the full text, llms-full.txt, is what the button copies; a model that reads the web can be told to read dsablic.github.io/sidou/llms.txt, which links the rest), then ask for what you hear in your head, like "a squelchy acid bass". It answers with a patch, its link and a few things to turn in LAB to shape it further: paste the answer here, or open the link it gives. Ask for several at once and it answers with a bank of up to sixteen, sent as one song, and ask for a whole song (a verse, a chorus, bass, drums and a lead, llms-song.txt) and it answers with a song the page previews, opens in the editor and sends. The page checks the patch with the FM-1's own rules, plays it, and sends it as a one-instrument song.

    The instructions The JSON Schema

    On the FM-1, after Send: menu, SONG, LOAD the song named after the sound, SEQ to INSTR, PRESETS to instrument 01 and ENV: it opens in LAB; SAVE keeps it as one of your USER sounds. The preview is the sound as the song player plays it; LAB plays all of it, its effects too. A patch link carries the patch after its #, which the browser never sends to a server; opening one checks and previews the patch here and sends nothing until you press Send to FM-1. Any of SIDOU's sounds above opens here as a patch with its JSON button, and a song's LAB sound with Patch beside its instrument (in section 5): give it to the model with the change you want. For one of your USER sounds: on the FM-1 load it in LAB and press REC, which puts it in the song, then Open the FM-1's current song below. The format: docs/reference/patch-json.md.

    5. A song: export

    The .sng is the song as the FM-1 saves it, SIDOU's settings kept in a trailer GoatTracker 2 skips over. The plain GoatTracker file leaves them out, for tools that refuse anything after the song; the page warns when GoatTracker would read it with another channel count. Each instrument downloads as GoatTracker's .ins (GTI5), its table rows with it.
