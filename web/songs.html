Songs for your FM-1

Back to the demo

A fresh SIDOU install has one song, the built-in SIDOU LOOP. These four showcase songs are original GoatTracker 2 songs written for SIDOU. Connect the FM-1 with its USB cable and send them from this page; afterwards pick one in the FM-1's menu: SONG, LOAD.

This browser cannot do this. Sending songs needs Web MIDI with SysEx, which Chrome and Edge have. Safari has no Web MIDI, and Firefox is not supported by this page. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Chrome on Android also works with a USB cable).

1. Connect

Plug in the FM-1 (running SIDOU 0.2.0 or newer, not in the updater) and press Connect. The browser asks to use your MIDI devices with SysEx: allow it. This page sends nothing until you press Connect, and then only to the pair you see selected below.

Not connected.

2. Send

    A song with the same name already on the FM-1 is replaced only after you confirm; an identical one is left alone. Songs sit in the FM-1's flash: delete one in the menu (SONG) to free room. Each song's .sng also opens in GoatTracker 2 (download link on its card).
