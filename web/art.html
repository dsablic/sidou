SCENE's RED MOON RITE draws its pictures from an art pack in the FM-1's store: two fighters with and without their swords, the flying and the stuck sword, three skies, the arena and the status bar. This page makes your own. It starts from the pack SIDOU ships; replace any slot with a PNG, watch it play, then download the .sidart or send it to the FM-1. The checks and the preview are SIDOU's own code running in the page: a pack it accepts, the FM-1 accepts.

A slot's PNG holds all its frames side by side in one row, each at the slot's size in stored pixels (the fighters and swords are stored at half width: each stored pixel is two on the screen). Take a slot's Template PNG, paint over it, and give it back with Replace. Colours are reduced to the screen's 16; a pixel under half opacity is transparent. The skies are painted whole: what equals the sky's gradient (the part the FM-1 computes) is left to the gradient.

    4. Send it to the FM-1

    This browser cannot reach the FM-1. That needs Web MIDI with SysEx (Chrome or Edge). Download the .sidart instead and send it from a browser that has it, or with sidou-sysex send.

    Plug in the FM-1 running SIDOU and press Connect: the browser asks to use your MIDI devices with SysEx. Nothing is sent until you press Send. The pack replaces the one on the FM-1; on the FM-1: menu, SCENE, RED MOON RITE.
