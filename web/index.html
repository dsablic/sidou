What this is

SIDOU is free firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket synth into a Commodore 64 SID instrument: an emulated SID chip (6581 or 8580) played from the FM-1's keys and knobs and over MIDI, with three effect slots after the chip. A step sequencer, a tracker and .sid playback are planned.

This page runs the latest release (or, run locally, your own build) in fm1-emu, an emulator of the FM-1's chip, in WebAssembly: the same update package (sidou.fwsc) a real FM-1 would take. SIDOU has not run on a real FM-1 yet.

How to play

Press Start (a browser plays sound only after a click). The title card shows for about a second; any key skips it.

A W S … ; 'the note keys, C4 to F5 (a piano row: W E T Y U O P are the sharps)
Z XOCT− / OCT+
1FX: the chip, 6581 or 8580
K1 K2 K3 K4cutoff, resonance, pulse width, preset (drag up / down or scroll; no keys); on the CHIP view (menu → CHIP) the focused section's: age, DAC, thump, bleed on the chip, a slot's four controls on a slot
5 held + K1–K4EDIT: the selected effect slot's Amount, A, B, Mix
2 / 3SCL: the next effect slot / ENV: its next algorithm
7HOME: the menu (← → SELECT moves, , . ALGORITHM changes)
8SAVE: MIDI learn
MASTERvolume (drag the knob at the top left)

The mouse or a touch screen plays the panel directly. All controls: docs/reference/controls.md.

MIDI in: pick a keyboard under MIDI in (Chrome, Edge, Firefox; Safari has no Web MIDI). SIDOU plays notes on every channel; CC 74 cutoff, 71 resonance, 1 pulse width, 7 volume, program change 0–3 the presets (MIDI map).

The emulator's limits

  • No USB. fm1-emu has no USB device, so no USB-MIDI in or out: MIDI from this page reaches SIDOU through the emulated TRS MIDI IN jack, and the panel's MIDI out goes nowhere.
  • No watchdog or P33. The power and reset block is not modelled, so a hang is not reset as it would be on the chip.
  • Instructions, not cycles. The emulator counts instructions; the clock setting is instructions per second. A real FM-1's timing can differ. The page starts at 240 MHz, the clock SIDOU's audio budget is checked at. If the status line's count of short audio quanta keeps climbing, your computer is behind: pick a lower clock (SIDOU may then run short of time itself, heavy effects first).
