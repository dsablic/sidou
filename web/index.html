What this is

SIDOU is free firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket synth into a Commodore 64 SID instrument: an emulated SID chip (6581 or 8580) played from the FM-1's keys and knobs and over MIDI, with three effect slots after the chip. It also plays and edits GoatTracker 2 songs with a step sequencer and a tracker (0 SEQ, Space PLAY): a new song comes with a SID drum kit and sampled digi drums, and STEP can play a step by chance, roll a new pattern (DICE) or vary one (MUTATE). On a real FM-1 the sound also goes out over USB as an audio input a computer can record. It plays PSID .sid tunes too, from the unit's flash, on its own 6502 (the menu's SIDS: one or two SID chips, PAL or NTSC, with mute and solo for the voices).

LAB (new in 1.0) is where you shape one sound and keep it: press 3 ENV, 4 LFO or 1 FX on LIVE. Five pages draw what their four knobs do; tap EDIT to compare A and B, EDIT + LFO undoes, EDIT + ARP varies, EDIT + SCL rolls a new voice, and 8 SAVE keeps the sound as a USER sound (PRESETS plays it after DICE). In this page the kept sounds go to the emulated flash like your songs.

This page runs the newest build of SIDOU's main branch, rebuilt whenever the firmware or this page changes, so it can be ahead of the latest release; the build line under the player names it (run locally: your own build) in fm1-emu, an emulator of the FM-1's chip, in WebAssembly: the same update package (sidou.fwsc) a real FM-1 would take. SIDOU runs on a real FM-1 too: see the latest release for installing it.

How to play

Press Start (a browser plays sound only after a click). Any key skips the boot.

The first-run tour. After the boot, SIDOU shows three large messages, one after another: TURN K1 (turn , ., or drag or scroll over the K1 knob), PRESS ENV (press 3, or click the ENV button: LAB opens at the sound's envelope) and HOLD HOME FOR HELP (hold 7, or hold the mouse on HOME, for a moment: a help card opens, LAB's here, and any control closes it; a tap on HOME goes back to LIVE). Each goes when you do what it asks, or after 8 seconds. On a real FM-1 the tour runs once, after an install, because the unit remembers it in its flash. This page keeps the emulated flash in your browser, one save per SIDOU build: the tour and the full boot come once per build, and what you save (settings, songs) is there on your next visit. Export… writes it to a file and Import… brings it back; More… → Reset saved flash starts over. Clearing the site's data in the browser removes it.

The mouse or a touch screen plays the panel directly (6 GLO and 9 ARP are the other buttons). All controls: docs/reference/controls.md.

MIDI in: pick a keyboard under MIDI in (Chrome, Edge, Firefox; Safari has no Web MIDI). SIDOU plays notes on every channel; CC 74 cutoff, 71 resonance, 1 pulse width, 7 volume, program change 0–3 the presets (MIDI map).

The emulator's limits

  • No tunes to play here. This page's flash starts with no .sid tunes (SIDS says NO TUNES), and with no USB it cannot take one: tunes go onto a real FM-1 over USB-MIDI or the TRS jack as a SysEx upload (the manual, with eleven openly licensed ones to start from).
  • No USB on this page. A real FM-1 running SIDOU has USB-MIDI in and out and sends its sound to a computer as a USB audio input; this page's player has no USB (fm1-emu emulates the FM-1's USB only in its desktop player, fm1-live --usb). MIDI from this page reaches SIDOU through the emulated TRS MIDI IN jack, and the panel's MIDI out goes nowhere.
  • No watchdog. The power and reset block's registers (P33) keep what SIDOU writes, but nothing acts on them, so a hang is not reset as it would be on the chip.
  • Instructions, not cycles. The emulator counts instructions; the clock setting is instructions per second. A real FM-1's timing can differ. The page starts at 240 MHz, the clock SIDOU's audio budget is checked at. If the status line's count of short audio quanta keeps climbing, your computer is behind: pick a lower clock (SIDOU may then run short of time itself, heavy effects first).
