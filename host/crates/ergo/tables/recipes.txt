# The example recipes: journeys written as executable how-tos (docs/reference/recipe-schema.md).
# `ergo --export-recipes docs/recipes` writes them as JSON (one file each, the machine form);
# `ergo --check-recipes docs/recipes` runs every JSON recipe in the emulator and fails one that no
# longer runs or no longer meets its expectations. Grammar: tables/journeys.txt's header, plus the
# recipe header lines under `journey` (goal, prereq, skills, related) and, on a step, the flags
#   do TEXT                  the control in panel terms, for the manual
#   value NAME MIN MAX       a parameter in a range (cutoff resonance pulse_width preset master ...)
#   absent TEXT              text not on the screen
#   big TEXT                 the text at 2x or larger: readable at arm's length
#   brighter darker louder quieter
#                            the sound against the 400 ms before the step
#   level MIN MAX            the last 400 ms's RMS in dBFS
# Every step has a caption and at least one expected outcome.

journey first-sound | First sound | poweron
  goal Make the synth sound the moment it is switched on.
  prereq The FM-1 running SIDOU, just switched on; nothing else plugged in but headphones or speakers.
  skills playing the keys; skipping the boot; reading LIVE's header
  related shape-a-sound; mute-and-solo
  step press-a-key | Press *any* key | No waiting for the boot: a key sounds at once, and LIVE takes over. | note A4 900 | sound; level -45 -8; do Press the note key marked A (the middle of the keys); focus A4; callout PRESS A KEY
  step boot-ends | Meet *LIVE* | The top line says the sound, SAW LEAD. Below: the three voices, the knobs and the chip. | wait 4500 | view live; text SAW LEAD; value octave 0 0; do Wait a moment: the title card ends by itself; screen 0 0 30 2; callout LIVE
  step play-a-chord | Three voices, *three* keys | SIDOU plays three voices, like the C64's chip. Hold a chord. | note C4 E4 G4 1800; wait 600 | sound; do Press three keys at once; screen 0 2 30 12; callout HOLD THREE KEYS
  step play-a-tune | Play a *tune* | The keys are F to G, two octaves and a bit. | play 120 A3:2 C4:2 E4:2 A4:2 G4:2 E4:2 C4:2 E4:2 A3:2 C4:2 E4:2 A4:2 G4:2 E4:2 C4:4 | sound; do Play a few notes; callout PLAY THE KEYS
  step octave-up | *OCT+* for higher | OCT+ shifts every key up an octave, and says so. | press OCT+; wait 200 | toast; text OCTAVE; value octave 1 1; do Press OCT+; screen 0 26 30 4; callout PRESS OCT+
  step play-higher | Up *there* | The same tune, an octave up. OCT- brings it back. | play 120 A3:2 C4:2 E4:2 A4:2 G4:2 E4:2 C4:4; wait 600 | sound; do Play a few notes; callout PLAY THE KEYS

journey shape-a-sound | Shape a sound with K1 to K4
  goal Open and close the filter, add resonance and change the sound while a chord holds.
  prereq On LIVE after the boot, with the first preset (SAW LEAD) playing.
  skills K1 cutoff; K2 resonance; PRESETS; holding a note while turning
  related first-sound; mute-and-solo
  step hold-a-note | Hold a *chord* | Hold three keys and keep holding them while you turn the knobs. | down A3; down C4; down E4; wait 1500 | sound; do Hold the note keys A, C and E down; callout HOLD A CHORD
  step close-the-filter | *K1* closes the filter | Turn K1 counter-clockwise, slowly: the sound goes dark and warm. | sweep K1 -60 3000; wait 1500 | darker; change; value cutoff 0 1100; do Turn K1 counter-clockwise about a third of a turn; screen 0 15 30 7
  step open-the-filter | And *opens* it | Turn it back up and past: bright again, all the way. | sweep K1 100 3000; wait 1200 | brighter; value cutoff 1100 2047; do Turn K1 clockwise about a full turn; screen 0 15 30 7
  step add-resonance | *K2*: resonance | K2 makes the filter ring at its cutoff. Sweep K1 again to hear it sing. | sweep K2 10 1200; sweep K1 -40 2000; sweep K1 40 2000 | change; value resonance 10 15; do Turn K2 clockwise about a quarter turn, then K1 down and up; screen 0 15 30 7; callout TURN K2, THEN K1
  step next-sound | *PRESETS*: another sound | The big knob picks the next preset, PWM BASS. Your edits to SAW LEAD are kept. | turn PRESETS 1; wait 2500 | text PWM BASS; value preset 1 1; sound; do Turn PRESETS one click clockwise; screen 0 0 30 2
  step let-go | Let *go* | Let go of the keys: the release rings out. | up A3; up C4; up E4; wait 1500 | silent; do Let go of the note keys; callout LET GO

journey chord-arpeggio | Chord arpeggios with ARP
  goal Play a chord as C64 tunes do: one voice stepping through its notes, up to fifty times a second.
  prereq On LIVE after the boot, with the first preset (SAW LEAD) playing.
  skills ARP; holding a chord; ARP held with K1, K2 and K3
  related first-sound; shape-a-sound
  step arp-on | Press *ARP* | ARP switches the chord arpeggio on. The badge under the band says so, large: a note every three frames, up, one octave. | press ARP; wait 800 | text ARP; text 3F UP 1OCT; value arp_on 1 1; do Press ARP; screen 0 27 30 2; focus ARP; callout PRESS ARP
  step hold-a-chord | Hold a *chord* | One voice plays all three notes in turn, one you can follow. The other two voices stay free. | down C4; down E4; down G4; wait 2500 | sound; do Hold the note keys C, E and G down; screen 0 2 30 12; callout HOLD A CHORD
  step faster | *ARP* + K1: the rate | Hold ARP and turn K1 back to 1F: a note every frame, fifty a second, the C64's buzzy chord. | holdturn ARP K1 -2; wait 2500 | value arp_rate 0 0; text 1F UP 1OCT; sound; do Hold ARP and turn K1 two clicks counter-clockwise; screen 0 14 30 7; focus K1; callout HOLD ARP + TURN K1
  step order | *ARP* + K2: the order | K2 turns it round: down, up and down, or as you played it. | holdturn ARP K2 2; wait 2500 | value arp_order 2 2; text 1F UD 1OCT; sound; do Hold ARP and turn K2 two clicks clockwise; screen 0 14 30 7; focus K2; callout HOLD ARP + TURN K2
  step span | *ARP* + K3: two octaves | K3 spreads the chord over two octaves. | holdturn ARP K3 1; wait 2500 | value arp_span 2 2; text 1F UD 2OCT; sound; do Hold ARP and turn K3 a click clockwise; screen 0 14 30 7; focus K3; callout HOLD ARP + TURN K3
  step let-go | Let *go* | Let go of the keys: the one voice's release rings out. | up C4; up E4; up G4; wait 1500 | silent; do Let go of the note keys; callout LET GO
  step arp-off | *ARP* again: off | The keys play chords on three voices again. ARP's settings stay with the preset. | press ARP; wait 800; note C4 E4 G4 1500 | absent ARP; value arp_on 0 0; sound; do Press ARP, then hold a chord; screen 0 2 30 19; focus ARP; callout PRESS ARP
